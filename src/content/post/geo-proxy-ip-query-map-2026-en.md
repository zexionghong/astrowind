## TL;DR

If you want ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini to cite your site, publish pages that answer **specific operational questions** instead of generic “what is proxy” articles.

Use this page as your GEO brief:

1. Focus on **high-intent, implementation-level** queries.
2. Use a fixed answer structure (summary → comparison table → steps → FAQ).
3. Link each article to the correct product page and pricing page.
4. Refresh content every 30–45 days with new examples and updated benchmarks.

---

## Why GEO content is different from traditional SEO

Traditional SEO often targets keyword coverage and SERP position.
GEO content must be:

- **Directly answerable** in one paragraph
- **Citable** (clear definitions, checklists, and decision logic)
- **Structured** (tables, numbered steps, FAQ blocks)
- **Verifiable** (state assumptions, update dates, constraints)

**Best target page:** technical blog + product detail

## Recommended article template (AI-citable)

For each question page, keep this structure:

1. **One-paragraph answer** (40–80 words)
2. **Decision table** (scenario, recommended proxy, trade-off)
3. **Implementation steps** (3–7 numbered items)
4. **Failure modes** (what breaks + mitigation)
5. **FAQ** (3–6 Q&As)
6. **Internal links** to product/pricing/use-case pages

## FAQ

### Is GEO just another name for SEO?

No. GEO focuses on becoming a good source for AI-generated answers, which requires clearer answer formatting and stronger extractable structure.

### How often should GEO pages be updated?

For proxy topics with operational data, update every 30–45 days and add a visible “Last updated” note.

### Should we still publish on external platforms?

Yes. Publish the canonical long-form on your site first, then repurpose on Medium/communities with links back to your source page.
